2. The game collects no data

Lunar Hopper does not collect, transmit or share any personal data or device data.

  • No registration or sign-in is required.
  • The game has no ads, in-app purchases, analytics or crash reporting.
  • The game does not connect to the internet and does not use developer servers or third-party services.

3. App permissions

The app requests no Android permissions, including internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or file access.

4. Data stored on your device

The game stores, only on your device: your progress (missions, records, credits, upgrades, achievements) and settings (sound, controls, language).

This data never leaves the device. Android backup is disabled for the game, so it is not included in cloud backups either.

You can delete your progress in the game: Settings, "Data & About" tab, "Reset progress". Uninstalling the game removes all of its data.

5. Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may process data related to downloading and installing the app as described in the Google Privacy Policy. The developer receives no data from Google that identifies you.

6. E-mail contact

If you e-mail us, we process your e-mail address and message only to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in handling correspondence). We keep it only as long as needed to handle your request.

7. Your rights

For data from correspondence, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability and to object. You may also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority (President of the Personal Data Protection Office): https://uodo.gov.pl.

8. Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

9. Changes

If the game starts using the internet, ads or other services that process data, this policy will be updated before such a version is released. The current version is available at the address listed on Google Play.
